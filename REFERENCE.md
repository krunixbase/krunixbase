## Organization:  

Krunixbase

## Purpose:  

Krunixbase is a security and compliance engineering organization focused on audit‑ready documentation, cryptographic validation, and evidence‑based technical workflows.

## Scope of Work: 

The organization operates within the domains of security and compliance documentation, audit‑ready evidence structures, cryptographic validation and reconstruction research, repository governance, licensing compliance, and the development of reproducible, defensible technical workflows.

## Repository Context:  

Krunixbase maintains publicly accessible reference repositories and controlled research laboratories, including licensing‑compliance, soc2‑evidence‑validator, and shamir_cracker_lab. These repositories collectively demonstrate documentation standards, evidence workflows, and controlled cryptographic research practices intended for institutional review.

## Usage Notice:  

All materials published under the Krunixbase organization are provided for research, documentation, and educational purposes only. They do not constitute legal, audit, or operational security consulting services. Professional assessment should be conducted by qualified auditors or advisors when required.
